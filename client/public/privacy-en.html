Operator: Egor Alekseevich Gerasimov, self-employed taxpayer, Saint Petersburg. The address for legally significant requests is not published pending legal consultation; this document must be updated after the address and processing procedure are confirmed.

1. Information handled

The website includes a short project brief used to prepare an enquiry. The entered information is not stored on the EffectGraff server and is not written to a website database. After submission, the visitor’s email application opens with a prepared message. The website does not request passport details, payment details or special categories of personal data.

If a visitor sends the prepared email or uses the email or phone link, further transmission and processing takes place in the relevant third-party service.

2. Purpose of contact

Contact details voluntarily provided in an enquiry may be used by EffectGraff only to respond about a commercial wall, mural, festival, open call, collaboration or related art project.

3. Cookies and analytics

The website asks for separate consent before using analytics. If the visitor selects “Reject”, the analytics script is not loaded. After consent, statistics are used to understand visits, page views and contact-link activity without advertising profiling or user accounts. For lead-generation measurement, it may record anonymous action types such as CTA, email, phone, VK profile, language-switch and brief-submission events; brief field contents and contact details are not sent to analytics. The VK community link is a normal outbound link and does not embed an external widget.

Questions about contact-data processing, correction, deletion or stopping use may be sent to the operator at Effectgraff@yandex.ru. The operator and website owner is Egor Alekseevich Gerasimov.
